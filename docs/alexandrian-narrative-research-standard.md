# Alexandrian Narrative Research Book

## Governing principle

> Read like a compelling book. Inspect like a research artifact.

An Alexandrian Narrative Research Book is engaging nonfiction with
textbook-grade evidence underneath it. The conversation is its narrative
engine:

- The user's actual starting belief establishes the point of departure.
- Questions, disagreements, and contradictions create tension.
- Research produces discoveries, connections, and reversals.
- Evidence changes—or fails to change—the argument.
- Unresolved questions open the next inquiry.

The workflow must never invent personal stakes, first-person authorship, or a
belief change that the transcript does not support.

## Chapter movement

A strong chapter makes these movements available without turning them into a
fixed heading template:

1. **Opening hook:** a consequential question, concrete situation,
   contradiction, or surprising grounded claim.
2. **Tension:** why the obvious answer is insufficient.
3. **Investigation:** ideas and evidence developing as an argument rather than
   an inventory of sources.
4. **Turn:** the strongest counterargument, unexpected connection, or evidence
   that changes direction.
5. **Synthesis:** what can now be concluded, what remains uncertain, and how
   the reader's model should change.
6. **Open door:** an implication or unresolved question that leads onward.

Chapter length, pacing, internal structure, and subheading density should vary
with the material.

## Prose standard

Use a recognizable authorial voice, varied sentence and paragraph rhythm,
concrete examples before abstraction, analogies that state their limits, and
smooth transitions between evidence and interpretation. Use pull quotes,
diagrams, marginal definitions, and subheadings only when they materially help
the reader.

Avoid generic AI-textbook scaffolding, including “In this chapter, we will
explore,” repetitive three-item lists, headings every few paragraphs, constant
recaps, disclaimer-heavy narration, one-source-per-paragraph exposition,
identical chapter shapes, and conclusions that merely repeat their openings.

## Evidence architecture

| Reading layer | Inspection layer |
|---|---|
| Natural narrative sentence | Exact grounded claim |
| Small evidence marker | Source receipt and verified location |
| Concise uncertainty statement | Full limitations record |
| Human-readable quotation | Verified verbatim passage |
| Smooth synthesis | Claim audit and provenance hash |

Evidence should remain available without dominating the reading experience.
Narrative freedom never permits stronger certainty, broader scope, invented
authority, flattened contradiction, or loss of provenance.

## Technical clarity boundary

ASD-STE100-inspired clarity may guide definitions, instructions, interface
copy, summaries, and technical procedures. It is not the primary voice of the
main chapters, and the Library does not claim ASD-STE100 compliance.
